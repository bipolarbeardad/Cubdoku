# Cubdoku blinded paper playtest

Place one bear in every row, column and lettered region. Bears cannot touch, even diagonally. Matching letters belong to the same region. Mark excluded squares with an X. Keep a separate mark grid if playing from a screen, or write marks next to the letters on paper.

Try the boards in the order shown. For another tester, rotate the order to reduce practice effects. Start a timer for each board. Note when you finish or stop, and briefly record your deductions where you get stuck. An organizer can verify completed grids after play. This packet tests reasoning without live game feedback or heart penalties.

After each board, rate perceived difficulty, fairness, clarity and how rewarding the advanced reasoning felt on a scale from 1 (low) to 5 (high). Record time, completion, hints, and guesses separately from ratings.
